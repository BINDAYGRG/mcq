UK Highway Code Quick Reference

Hierarchy of Road Users (Rules H1, H2, H3) & Stopping Distances

Rule H1: Hierarchy of Road Users

Drivers of vehicles that can cause the greatest harm (HGVs, cars, motorcycles) have the greatest responsibility to reduce danger to more vulnerable road users: Pedestrians > Cyclists > Horse Riders > Motorcyclists > Cars/HGVs.

Rule H2: Pedestrians Crossing at Junctions

At a junction, drivers MUST give way to pedestrians who are crossing OR waiting to cross a road into or from which you are turning. You must also give way at zebra/parallel crossings.

Rule H3: Priority for Cyclists & Horse Riders

Drivers MUST NOT cut across cyclists, horse riders or horse-drawn vehicles when turning into or out of a junction or changing lanes. Wait for a safe gap as if they were a motor vehicle.
